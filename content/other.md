## Some good reads

- Anything by [El Gato y la Caja](https://elgatoylacaja.com/)
- [You must not "do your own research" when it comes to science](https://www.forbes.com/sites/startswithabang/2020/07/30/you-must-not-do-your-own-research-when-it-comes-to-science/)
- *The Real Politics of the Horn of Africa: Money, War and the Business of Power* by Alex de Waal
- Anything by Sir Terry Pratchett — if you're looking for a place to start, try *Mort*, *Equal Rites* or the traditional introduction to the Discworld, *The Colour of Magic*
- *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* and its sequels, by Douglas Adams
- Any play by Oscar Wilde — you might start with *The Importance of Being Earnest*
- Anything by J.R.R. Tolkien, though I suggest starting with *The Lord of the Rings*
- All of Jorge Luis Borges' short stories (may I recommend *El laberinto* / *The Labyrinth*)
