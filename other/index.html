Some Good Reads

  • Anything by El Gato y la Caja
  • You Must Not ‘Do Your Own Research’ When It Comes To Science
  • The Real Politics of the Horn of Africa: Money, War and the Business of Power by Alex de Waal
  • Anything by Sir Terry Pratchett – If you’re looking for a place to start consider: Mort, Equal Rites or the more traditional introduction to the Discworld: The Colour of Magic
  • Hitchhiker’s Gide to the Galaxy, and its sequels, by Douglas Adams
  • Any play by Oscar Wilde, you may start with The Importance of Being Earnest
  • Anything by JRR Tolkien, although I do suggest starting with The Lord of the Rings
  • All of Jorge Luis Borges’ short stories (may I recommend: El Laberinto / The Labyrinth)
